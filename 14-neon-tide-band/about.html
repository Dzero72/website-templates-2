Four friends and a broken drum machine

Neon Tide formed in 2017 in a rehearsal room above a fishmonger. Isla, Kofi, Wren and Dev wrote their first single around a drum machine that would only play one pattern.

Low Sun, their third album, was recorded live to tape over ten days on the Isle of Mull.

  • Isla Kerr

    Vocals and synths.

  • Kofi Mensah and Wren Aldous

    Guitar and bass.

  • Dev Malhotra

    Drums and that drum machine.
