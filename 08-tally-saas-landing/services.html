What Tally does

Tally sends your invoices, reminds late payers politely and matches payments to your books, so freelancers and small studios get paid nine days sooner.

  • Send in a minute

    Turn a quote or a tracked hour into an invoice with one click.

  • Polite reminders

    Scheduled nudges written in your voice, paused the moment a client pays.

  • Pay by link

    Clients pay by card or bank transfer without creating an account.

  • Automatic matching

    Incoming payments are matched to invoices and marked as paid.

  • Tax-ready reports

    Quarterly summaries your accountant can open without asking questions.

  • Works with your tools

    Two-way sync with the accounting and time-tracking apps you already use.
