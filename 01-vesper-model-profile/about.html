Seven years in front of the camera

Mara started modeling at nineteen after being scouted at a student fashion show in Chicago. Since then she has worked across four fashion capitals, with a focus on tailoring, knitwear and beauty.

She trained in contemporary dance for a decade, which shows in how she moves on a runway and how quickly she finds a shape in front of a lens.
