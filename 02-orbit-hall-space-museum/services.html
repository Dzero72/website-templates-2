On view now

Orbit Hall is a museum of spaceflight with four galleries, a 22-metre planetarium dome and a flown crew capsule you can walk right up to.

  • The Launch Floor

    A full-size booster stage laid on its side, with the engines at eye level.

    Gallery 1

  • Capsule Kestrel 4

    The scorched heat shield and cabin of a capsule that orbited for 96 days.

    Gallery 2

  • Living in Orbit

    Sleep stations, a galley and a working airlock door to try for yourself.

    Gallery 3

  • Planetarium dome

    Live-narrated sky shows every hour, with a late show on Fridays.

    45 minutes

  • Moon rock table

    Touch a slice of lunar basalt and compare it with rock from Earth.

    Gallery 4

  • Mission control

    Run a simulated launch from a restored row of 1970s consoles.

    Ages 8 and up

How it works

  1. Pick a time slot

    Tickets are timed on weekends and school holidays.

  2. Add a dome show

    Seats are limited to 140 per show, so reserve ahead.

  3. Arrive ten minutes early

    Bag check is at the east entrance beside the rail spur.
