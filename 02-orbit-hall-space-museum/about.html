Built in a hangar that once assembled satellites

Orbit Hall opened in 2011 in a decommissioned assembly hangar. The cranes and clean-room doors are still in place, and most of the collection arrived by the same rail spur that once carried satellites out.

We are an independent charity. Ticket sales fund conservation work and free school visits for more than 30,000 students a year.

  • Hands on

    Wherever conservation allows, objects are out of their cases.

  • Free for schools

    Every state school visit is free, including the dome show.

  • Working collection

    Our restoration bay is open to visitors on weekends.
