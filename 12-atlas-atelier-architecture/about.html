A small studio that draws everything twice

Founded by Ingrid Sollis and Paolo Yeung in 2012, the studio works on four or five buildings at a time. Every scheme is tested as a physical model before it is drawn in detail.

We prefer materials that weather rather than wear out, and we return to each building after one year and after five to see how it is doing.

  • Models first

    Card, timber and plaster, at 1:50 and 1:5.

  • Low-carbon structure

    Timber frames wherever spans allow.

  • We come back

    Post-occupancy visits at one and five years.
