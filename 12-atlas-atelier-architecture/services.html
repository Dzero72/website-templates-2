Built work

Atlas Atelier designs houses, schools and small public buildings in timber, brick and concrete. Nine people, one room, every project modelled by hand.

    A long timber house on a flood plain, raised on brick piers.

  • Wren Primary School

    Eight classrooms around a covered courtyard.

    Education, 2023

  • Quarry Baths

    Public pool and sauna cut into a disused quarry.

    Civic, 2022

    Workplace, 2022
