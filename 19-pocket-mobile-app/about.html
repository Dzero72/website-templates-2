Made by four people and two hundred plants

Pocket Garden started in 2022 when a botanist and a developer shared a flat full of dying ferns. The first version was a spreadsheet taped to the fridge.

Care plans are written by horticulturists and checked against published research. We never sell your data or show adverts.
