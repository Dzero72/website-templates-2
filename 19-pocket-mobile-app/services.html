What the app does

Pocket Garden learns each plant's light and watering needs, then reminds you only when something needs doing.

  • Identify from a photo

    Point your camera at a leaf to name the plant and load its care plan.

  • Reminders that adapt

    Watering schedules shift with the season and your local weather.

  • Light meter

    Measure a windowsill with your phone to find the right spot.

  • Plant doctor

    Describe yellow leaves or brown tips and get a likely cause.

  • Shared homes

    Housemates see the same list, so nothing gets watered twice.

  • Holiday mode

    Send a simple care sheet to whoever is plant-sitting.

How it works

  1. Download

    Available for iPhone and Android.

  2. Add your plants

    Snap a photo of each one.

  3. Follow the nudges

    One short list each morning, and only when needed.
