A merchant's house, opened to guests

Casa Solane was built in 1894 for a family of citrus traders. The Solane family reopened it in 2018 after a four-year restoration using the original tiles and shutters.

The kitchen cooks from the garden and the morning fish market. Dinner is one long table, three nights a week.

  • The lemon garden

    Sixty trees, a long table and shade all afternoon.

  • Saltwater pool

    Heated by the sun and open from seven until dusk.

  • The kitchen

    Breakfast daily, and dinner on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday.
