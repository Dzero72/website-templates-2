One fire, lit at noon

Chef Lena Marsh opened Ember & Rye in 2019 in a former bakery. The original brick oven is still the only heat source in the kitchen.

We buy whole animals and whole fields, which is why the menu is short and why it changes every week.

  • Wood fire only

    No gas and no induction. Everything touches the hearth.

  • Nine farms

    We print their names on the back of every menu.

    Twelve dishes and thirty wines, chosen carefully.
