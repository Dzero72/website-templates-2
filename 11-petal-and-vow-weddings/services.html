Ways we can help

Petal & Vow plans twenty weddings a year across the lake district, from a first venue visit to the last sparkler.

  • Full planning

    Everything from budget and venue to the morning-after brunch.

  • Partial planning

    You have the venue and the date. We bring it all together from there.

  • On-the-day coordination

    We take over six weeks out and run the day to your plan.

  • Floral design

    Seasonal flowers grown within fifty miles and arranged in our studio.

  • Elopements

    Two people, one view, a celebrant and a very good picnic.

  • Destination weekends

    Welcome dinners, the wedding and a farewell lunch, fully hosted.

How it works

  1. Check your date

    We reply within two days to say whether we are free.

  2. Meet for coffee

    An hour to hear what you have in mind.

  3. Receive a proposal

    A plan and a fixed fee, sent within a week.
