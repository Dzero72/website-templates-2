On the roast schedule

Kiln is a small roastery and coffee bar. We buy from six farms we have visited and roast twice a week in fifteen-kilo batches.

  • Finca Aurora, Colombia

    Washed caturra. Red apple, caramel and a clean finish.

    $19 / 250g

  • Guji Hambela, Ethiopia

    Natural heirloom. Blueberry, jasmine and cocoa.

    $22 / 250g

  • Kiln House Espresso

    A blend built for milk. Hazelnut, toffee and dark chocolate.

    $17 / 250g

  • Santa Elena, Honduras

    Honey process. Orange peel, almond and brown sugar.

    $18 / 250g

  • Night Shift Decaf

    Sugarcane process. Fig, malt and milk chocolate.

    $18 / 250g

  • Roaster's subscription

    Two new bags picked by us, sent every fortnight.

    $32 / delivery
