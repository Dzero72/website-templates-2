Started around one kitchen table

In 2012 a retired teacher, Joan Petrie, began inviting newly arrived families to dinner. By the end of the year thirty neighbors were doing the same.

Today Open Harbor has eleven staff and more than six hundred volunteers. Our accounts are audited and published every year.

  • Neighbors first

    Relationships do the work that services cannot.

  • Led by experience

    Half of our board arrived as refugees.

  • Open books

    Annual reports and audits are free to download.
