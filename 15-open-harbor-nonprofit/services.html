Our programs

Open Harbor pairs refugee and migrant families with local volunteers for their first year in the city, covering housing, language, school and work.

  • Welcome partners

    A trained volunteer meets each family weekly for twelve months.

  • English at the kitchen table

    Small conversation classes held in people's homes.

  • First job fund

    Covers licences, tools and transport for a first job.

  • School bridge

    Tutors and interpreters for children starting school mid-year.

  • Housing desk

    Help with leases, deposits and tenant rights.

  • Community suppers

    A shared meal on the last Friday of every month.

How it works

  1. Give

    A monthly gift of $25 funds one conversation class.

  2. Volunteer

    Four hours of training, then two hours a week.

  3. Partner

    Employers and landlords can join our network.
